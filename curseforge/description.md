# Throwing Disc Legacy

A combat and utility weapon mod that recreates the upcoming 2027 Minecraft Throwing Disc weapon, along with dedicated enchantments and expanded mechanics.

## Features

- **5 Disc Tiers**: Craft and upgrade **Copper**, **Iron**, **Golden**, **Diamond**, and **Netherite** throwing discs, each with distinct durability, melee stats, thrown damage, flight speed, range, and bounce count.
- **Ricochet & Direct Return**: Thrown discs slice through enemies, ricochet off solid blocks, deflect off shields and deflecting entities, and fly straight back to you at a steady speed.
- **Slot Reservation Placeholder**: Reserving the original main-hand or off-hand slot while the disc is in flight prevents picked-up drops from stealing your throwing hand.
- **Dispenser Support**: Throwing discs can also be launched directly from dispensers.
- **Structure Loot Integration**: Configurable loot injection into Trial Chambers, Village Weaponsmiths, Bastion Treasures, End Cities, and Buried Treasures.
- **Enchantment Descriptions Compatible**: Full localization support for Enchantment Descriptions and similar tooltip mods.

## Custom Enchantments

- **Ricochet**: Increases maximum surface bounces and flight range, while boosting damage with each bounce.
- **Tailwind**: Increases the flight speed of throwing discs.
- **Returning Edge**: Deals bonus slicing damage to targets hit while the disc is returning.
- **Magnetic Recall**: Pulls nearby item drops and XP orbs along the disc's path, and allows sneaking to recall the disc early.
- **Cleaving Harvest**: Allows thrown or held discs to harvest blocks matching their material tier, carrying thrown block drops and XP back to your hand.
- **Vein Harvest**: Works with Cleaving Harvest to chain-mine connected ores of the same type and return their drops.

## Configuration

All disc stats, flight mechanics, enchantment values, loot toggles, slot placeholder behavior, and client trail/spin rendering options can be customized in the mod configs.

# Throwing Disc Legacy（投掷圆盘）

本模组是对 2027 年即将推出的 Minecraft 圆盘武器（Throwing Disc）进行的复刻，并在此基础上加入了专属附魔与配套机制。

## 核心特性

- **5 种材质圆盘**：提供**铜、铁、金、钻石、下界合金** 5 个等级的投掷圆盘，各自拥有独立的耐久、近战属性、投掷伤害、飞行速度、射程与弹射次数。
- **表面弹射与直线回旋**：圆盘可穿透命中多个目标、在坚硬方块表面连续弹射、在盾牌等目标上触发偏转，并在飞行结束或召回时从当前位置直线匀速飞回手中。
- **防抢手栏位占位**：投掷后在原主手或副手栏位生成占位物品，防止地面掉落物或带回的战利品占满手持栏位，圆盘返回后自动归位。
- **发射器支持**：支持放入发射器中作为投射物发射。
- **结构战利品箱生成**：可在试炼密室、村庄武器匠、堡垒遗迹宝藏、末地城与埋藏宝藏箱中生成（均可在配置中独立开关）。
- **附魔描述兼容**：内置完整的中英文附魔名称与描述键，兼容 Enchantment Descriptions 等附魔描述模组。

## 专属附魔

- **弹射（Ricochet）**：增加最大表面弹射次数与飞行射程，且每次弹射后提升伤害。
- **疾旋（Tailwind）**：提升投掷圆盘的飞行速度。
- **回旋斩（Returning Edge）**：强化圆盘在返程阶段命中目标时造成的切割伤害。
- **磁吸召回（Magnetic Recall）**：吸附沿途掉落物与经验球并带回，飞行途中潜行可提前召回圆盘。
- **旋刃采掘（Cleaving Harvest）**：允许投掷或手持圆盘采掘符合材质等级的方块，投掷破坏时会将方块掉落物与经验直接带回手中。
- **连锁采集（Vein Harvest）**：配合旋刃采掘生效，破坏矿石时自动连锁采掘周围相连的同类矿石并将战利品带回。

## 配置与定制

所有圆盘的基础属性、飞行速度与射程、附魔数值、战利品开关、栏位占位机制以及客户端自转与轨迹粒子效果均可在配置文件中自由调整。
